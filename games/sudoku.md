**Sudoku - Classic Sudoku Puzzle** is a free classic sudoku game by Humble Logic Games. Fill the 9×9 grid so that every row, column and 3×3 box contains the digits 1 to 9. Thousands of puzzles across several difficulty levels, from easy to expert, keep your brain sharp a few minutes at a time.

## What the game does

- Classic 9×9 sudoku puzzles with multiple difficulty levels.
- Pencil notes, hints, auto-check and undo.
- Statistics and daily challenges to track your progress.
- Play offline, anytime, with no account required.

## Why the game asks for your Google account

Sudoku - Classic Sudoku Puzzle offers optional sign-in through **Google Play Games Services**. When you sign in, the game uses your Google account only to:

- record your achievements and leaderboard scores,
- save your progress and statistics to the cloud (Play Games "Saved Games") so you can continue on another device or after reinstalling.

The game never reads your email, contacts or any other Google data, and it works fully without signing in.
