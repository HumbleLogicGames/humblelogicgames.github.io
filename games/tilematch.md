**Tile Match Sweet Triple Match** is a free, relaxing triple-match puzzle game by Humble Logic Games. Tap three identical tiles to match them and clear every layer of the board. Hundreds of hand-made levels, colorful themes and gentle difficulty make it easy to pick up and hard to put down.

Published on Google Play as "Tile Match Sweet -Triple Match".

## What the game does

- Match three identical tiles to remove them from the board.
- Clear all tiles on a level to move on to the next one.
- Use hints, shuffles and undo when you get stuck.
- Play offline, anytime, with no account required.

## Why the game asks for your Google account

Tile Match Sweet Triple Match offers optional sign-in through **Google Play Games Services**. When you sign in, the game uses your Google account only to:

- record your achievements and leaderboard scores,
- save your level progress to the cloud (Play Games "Saved Games") so you can continue on another device or after reinstalling.

The game never reads your email, contacts or any other Google data, and it works fully without signing in.
