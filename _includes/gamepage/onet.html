Onet Connect - Pair Matching

Spot two matching tiles and connect them.

Quick eyes and a little strategy go a long way in this fast, cheerful pair-matching puzzle.

Cute animals, fresh fruit, cakes and toys. Thousands of pictures turn up level by level.

How to play

  1. Tap two matching tiles to connect them.
  2. The line between them can turn at most twice and can't cross other tiles.
  3. Clear the board before time runs out.

Features

  • Simple rules, fast rounds
  • Thousands of tile pictures
  • Saves automatically
  • Plays offline
