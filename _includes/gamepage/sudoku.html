Sudoku - Classic Sudoku Puzzle

The classic nine-by-nine, done cleanly.

No clutter, no noise. Just you, a pencil mark or two, and that quiet click when the last number drops into place.

Start with gentle warm-ups and work your way up to expert grids that really make you think. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

How to play

  1. Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the numbers 1 to 9.
  2. Each number appears only once in every row, column and box.
  3. Use pencil marks to note the numbers a cell could still take.

Features

  • Five difficulty levels, from Picnic to Expert
  • Smart hints that teach you the technique
  • Unlimited undo and redo
  • Plays offline, saves automatically
