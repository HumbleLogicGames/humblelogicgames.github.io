Amaze OUT!

One more level. Just one more.

A minimalist logic puzzle. Tap arrows to slide them out of the grid, in the right order, one by one.

No timers, no coins, no power-ups. Just clean logic and that moment when the whole board clicks.

How to play

  1. Tap an arrow to slide it the way it points.
  2. An arrow with another in its way has to wait its turn.
  3. Clear every arrow to solve the level.

Features

  • Thousands of hand-curated levels
  • Small grids to huge ones
  • Custom colors, dark and light mode
  • Plays offline
