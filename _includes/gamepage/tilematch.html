Tile Match Sweet Triple Match

Tap three matching tiles and watch them pop.

Sweet colors, calm pacing and level after level to clear. It's the kind of puzzle you open for one level and close forty levels later.

Easy to start, and the boards grow trickier the further you go.

How to play

  1. Tap a tile to move it into the tray at the bottom.
  2. Three of the same tile in the tray clear together.
  3. Clear the board before the tray fills up.
