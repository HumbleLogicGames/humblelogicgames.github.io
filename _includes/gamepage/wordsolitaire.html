Word Solitaire : Bloom

A cozy word game with a solitaire soul.

Flip cards from the deck and group the words by their hidden connection. No timer, no pressure, just the small joy of "of course they go together".

It feels like a word association puzzle and a game of solitaire at the same time.

How to play

  1. Flip cards from the deck one at a time.
  2. Spot the hidden connection between the words.
  3. Group them into the right category to clear it.
  4. Clear every category to finish the level.

Features

  • Hundreds of hand-made categories
  • No timer, play at your own pace
  • No ads in the middle of a level
  • Plays offline
