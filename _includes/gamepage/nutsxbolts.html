Nuts and Bolts : Screw Puzzle

Twist, pull and drop.

Figure out which screw comes out first and watch the plates fall away. A tidy little brain teaser that rewards careful thinking.

Each level adds new pieces and new ways to get stuck, and a satisfying moment when it all comes apart.

How to play

  1. Tap a screw to pull it out, then tap an empty hole to put it back in.
  2. Free every plate so it drops off the board.
  3. Plan the order. One wrong screw can block the way.

Features

  • Hundreds of hand-built levels
  • Steady difficulty curve
  • Hints when you're stuck
  • Plays offline
