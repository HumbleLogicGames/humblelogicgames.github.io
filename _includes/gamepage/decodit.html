DECODiT - Decrypt Crossword

Crack the code, reveal the quote.

A word puzzle in the spirit of the classic cryptogram. Every letter has been swapped for a number. Work out which is which and the hidden sentence appears.

Each solved puzzle reveals a fun fact, and you pick up new English words along the way.

How to play

  1. Each number stands for one letter.
  2. Fill in a letter and it appears everywhere that number is used.
  3. Use the words you can guess to crack the rest.

Features

  • Fun facts in every puzzle
  • Great for building English vocabulary
  • Hints when you're stuck
  • Plays offline
