Spider Solitaire

Eight columns, a full deck and one good plan.

Start with one suit to unwind, or take on four suits when you want a real challenge. Every cleared run feels earned.

Big, easy-to-read cards, tap-to-move and daily challenges that keep you coming back.

How to play

  1. Build runs from King down to Ace in the same suit.
  2. A full run from King to Ace clears itself from the table.
  3. Deal a new row from the stock when you are stuck.

Features

  • 1, 2 and 4 suit difficulties
  • Daily challenges with trophies
  • Unlimited hints and undo
  • Right- or left-handed layout
  • Plays offline
