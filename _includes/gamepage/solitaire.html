Solitaire : Classic Card Games

Klondike solitaire, the way it should feel.

The card game everyone knows, made smooth, fast and satisfying. Deal, stack and clear the table one smart move at a time.

Turn one card for a relaxed game or three for a real test, and choose winnable deals when you want a game that can always be solved.

How to play

  1. Build down the columns in alternating colors, King to Ace.
  2. Move Aces to the foundations and build each suit up to the King.
  3. Draw from the stock when you run out of moves.

Features

  • Draw 1 and Draw 3
  • Winnable deals
  • Smart hints and unlimited undo
  • Vegas scoring and personal statistics
  • Custom card faces, backs and backgrounds
