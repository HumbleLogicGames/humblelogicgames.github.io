Bubble Pop Burst: Bubble Shoot

Aim, bounce and burst.

Line up your shots, clear the colors and drop whole clusters at once. Simple to play, hard to put down.

Plan your angle off the walls, and every level asks for a slightly different strategy.

How to play

  1. Drag to aim and release to shoot.
  2. Match three or more bubbles of the same color to pop them.
  3. Pop the bubbles holding a cluster up and the whole cluster falls.

Features

  • Hundreds of levels
  • Daily missions and rewards
  • Bank shots off the walls
  • Plays offline
