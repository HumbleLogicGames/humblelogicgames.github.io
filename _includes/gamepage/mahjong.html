Mahjong Solitaire Classic

A timeless tile puzzle, calm and clear.

Find matching pairs, open up the layout and clear the whole stack. It looks simple, and then the stack starts asking you to plan ahead.

More than a thousand boards, from small warm-ups to tall towers.

How to play

  1. Tap two matching tiles to remove them.
  2. A tile is free when nothing sits on top of it and its left or right side is open.
  3. Clear every tile to finish the board.

Features

  • Over 1,000 boards
  • Free hints and unlimited undo
  • Several layouts and backgrounds
  • Plays offline on phone and tablet
