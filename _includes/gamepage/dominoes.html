Dominoes LoFi: Domino Online

Classic dominoes on a cozy pixel-art 3D table.

▶ Play in browser

Free in your browser · get the app for the full game

The tile game you grew up with, in a warm low-fi world. Play a quick round against friendly AI, team up two against two, or open a private room and send the link to a friend.

Six rivals with their own styles, and every one of them plays fair. None of them ever peeks at your tiles.

How to play

  1. Match the numbers on the open ends to grow the line.
  2. Can't play? Draw from the boneyard or pass, depending on the mode.
  3. Empty your hand first to win the round.
  4. Reach the target score to win the match.

Features

  • Draw, Block and All Fives
  • Online quick match, private rooms and 2v2 teams
  • Six fair AI rivals, from Rookie to Iris
  • Play in the browser or in the app
