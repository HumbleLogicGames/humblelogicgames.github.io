# DOMINOES LOFI PRIVACY DETAILS

This page supplements the [HumbleLogicGames Privacy Policy](/docs/privacypolicy) for Dominoes LoFi. For this game, it takes precedence over the general retention and verification terms in that policy. Dominoes LoFi does not have user accounts.

This page was last updated on Nov 11, 2026.

**What our game server stores.** If you play online, open the leaderboard, send feedback, or play with game logging enabled, we store a random install ID, your display name and avatar, your approximate country, your device platform and app version, match results, and game logs of the moves played. We do not store your IP address with these records.

**Analytics.** We use Google Analytics for Firebase in the mobile apps and Google Analytics in the web version (including the version inside Toss) to understand how the game is played. It records events such as app opens, session length, matches started, finished, or left early, the game mode and AI opponent you chose, match results, and ads shown. We set your random install ID as the analytics user ID, so we can link this analytics data with the game logs on our server. We use the linked data to measure retention and to tune AI difficulty for all players. We do not send your display name, email address, or precise location to Google Analytics. The web version uses Google Analytics cookies. The version on Poki does not use Google Analytics.

**How long we keep it.**
- Game logs and feedback are deleted automatically after 90 days.
- Player records are deleted 400 days after your last connection unless you delete them sooner.
- Weekly leaderboards are deleted after 40 days. All-time leaderboard entries are kept for up to 5 years unless you delete them sooner.
- Reports about offensive player names are kept for as long as necessary to enforce our rules and prevent abuse. They identify players by a one-way hashed ID and include the reported display name.
- Google Analytics user-level data is kept for 2 months. Copies we export for analysis are identified only by the install ID and are deleted after 90 days. We may keep aggregated statistics that do not identify any install ID.
- Server backups are overwritten within 7 days.

**How to delete your data.** Open OPTIONS and tap DELETE MY DATA. This immediately removes the player records and leaderboard entries linked to your install ID from our server, and clears your statistics, profile, block list, and install ID from your device. You do not need to contact us or complete the verification process in the Privacy Policy. Your device must be connected to the internet, and you must not be in an online room.

**What the button does not delete.** Game logs and feedback already received are not removed individually and expire after 90 days. Report records are retained as described above. Analytics data already sent is not removed individually and expires as described above, but after you delete your data, your device gets a new install ID, so new analytics data is no longer linked to your earlier data. Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Analytics is handled under Google's own policies; you can limit advertising use through your device's privacy settings and the in-app consent form where it is shown.
